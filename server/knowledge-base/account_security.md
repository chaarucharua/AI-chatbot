# NovaCart Account Security & Privacy

## Account Protection & Authentication
NovaCart adheres to industry-leading cybersecurity practices to protect your personal and financial information:
- Password Requirements: Must be at least 8 characters long, containing a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Available under Account Settings > Security. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) and SMS OTP codes.
- Session Management: You can view and revoke active sessions across multiple devices at any time.

## Password Reset Procedure
If you forget your password:
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. You will receive a secure, one-time verification link valid for 15 minutes.
4. Set your new password. For security, all active sessions on other devices will be terminated automatically upon reset.

## Payment Security
NovaCart never stores raw credit card numbers or CVV codes on our servers. All transactions are tokenized and processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways.

## Account Deletion & Data Privacy
In compliance with GDPR and CCPA regulations, you have full ownership of your data. You may request a machine-readable export of your personal data or request permanent deletion of your NovaCart account under Settings > Privacy & Data. Account deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
