# NovaCart Returns & Refund Policy

## 30-Day Return Window
At NovaCart, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. You may return most eligible items within 30 days of the recorded delivery date for a full refund to your original payment method.

## Eligibility & Condition
To qualify for a refund:
- Items must be in their original, unused condition, including all original packaging, tags, accessories, manuals, and protective inserts.
- Sealed electronics, software, and consumable goods must have their factory shrink-wrap or security seals intact.
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" are non-returnable unless defective or damaged upon arrival.

## Return Shipping & Fees
- Standard returns: A pre-paid printable return shipping label is provided through your NovaCart account portal. A flat return shipping fee of $5.99 will be deducted from your total refund amount.
- Defective, damaged, or incorrect items: Return shipping is 100% free. NovaCart will cover all shipping expenses and waive any restocking fees.

## Refund Processing Timeline
Once our warehouse receives and inspects your returned package (typically 3 to 5 business days after carrier scan), your refund will be authorized. Refunds are credited back to your original payment method:
- Credit / Debit Cards: 3 to 7 business days, depending on your financial institution.
- PayPal / Digital Wallets: 1 to 2 business days.
- NovaCart Store Credit / Gift Cards: Instant balance update.

## Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. If you need a different size, color, or model, please return the unwanted item for a refund and place a new order on NovaCart at your convenience.
