# NovaCart Warranty & Product Guarantee

## Standard 1-Year Limited Warranty
Every brand-new physical product purchased directly on NovaCart includes our comprehensive 1-Year Limited Warranty against manufacturer defects in materials and craftsmanship, effective from the date of initial delivery.

## What Is Covered
- Hardware failures and electrical defects under normal household or intended use.
- Premature component degradation not caused by accidental damage or neglect.
- Factory assembly flaws and out-of-the-box non-functioning units.

## What Is Not Covered
- Cosmetic blemishes, minor scratches, and regular wear-and-tear resulting from ordinary usage.
- Damage caused by liquid submersion, drops, impacts, electrical surges, or unauthorized modifications.
- Consumable parts that diminish over time (e.g., standard disposable batteries).

## NovaCart Care+ Extended Protection
Customers can upgrade to NovaCart Care+ during checkout or within 30 days of product purchase. Care+ extends coverage to 2 or 3 years and includes:
- Up to two accidental damage claims per 12-month period (subject to a nominal $29 deductible).
- Priority 24/7 dedicated telephone and live chat support with expedited product replacement.

## How to File a Warranty Claim
1. Log in to your NovaCart account and navigate to "My Orders".
2. Locate the eligible item and click "File Warranty Claim".
3. Provide a brief description of the defect, serial number, and attach a clear photo or video showing the issue.
4. Our technical support team reviews claims within 24 to 48 hours and issues a free return shipping label or direct replacement authorization.
