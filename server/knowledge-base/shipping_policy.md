# NovaCart Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Domestic Shipping Options
NovaCart offers three tiers of domestic shipping within the continental United States:
1. Standard Shipping: Delivered within 3 to 5 business days. Free on orders over $50; otherwise, a flat rate of $4.99 applies.
2. Expedited Shipping: Delivered within 2 business days. Flat rate of $9.99 for all orders.
3. Overnight Priority: Next-business-day delivery for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST Monday through Friday. Flat rate of $19.99.

## Processing Times
Orders are processed and packaged at our fulfillment centers Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays. Most orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation. During peak holiday seasons (November 15 to December 31), processing may take up to 72 hours.

## Order Tracking
Once your package ships, you will receive an email containing your tracking number and carrier link (UPS, FedEx, or USPS). You can also track your shipment live by navigating to your NovaCart account under "Orders" and selecting "Track Package". Tracking details typically update within 12 hours of courier pickup.

## International Shipping
NovaCart currently ships to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, and New Zealand. International delivery typically takes 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance. Customs duties, taxes, and import fees are calculated and collected at checkout so there are no surprise fees upon delivery.

## Damaged or Lost Shipments
If your tracking status indicates "Delivered" but you have not received your parcel, please check with neighbors and building management first. If it cannot be located within 24 hours of the delivery timestamp, contact NovaCart support immediately. We will initiate a carrier investigation and dispatch a replacement or issue a full refund within 3 business days.
